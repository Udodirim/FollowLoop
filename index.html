How to Use FollowLoop

FollowLoop helps you prepare, check and act on reminders and follow-ups without letting things slip.
Add→Check→See what’s due→Review→Act→Follow up
1

Add your information

Connect a Google Sheet, paste rows from Excel or Google Sheets into Chat, or add a reminder manually.
2

Let FollowLoop check it

FollowLoop flags missing names, contacts, dates, times or messages before you act.
3

See what needs attention

Use the Dashboard to see what is due today, upcoming or overdue.
4

Review

Choose Email, WhatsApp or Calendar and check the message before continuing.
5

Complete the action

FollowLoop prepares the action. You review and complete the send or calendar step.
6

Use bulk actions

Tick people or choose Everyone, a Group, Due Today or Overdue.
7

Use Chat

Try: “Remind Emmanuel about Saturday’s meeting at 4 PM” or “WhatsApp everyone due today.”

Advanced: Content Queue

Prepare recurring communication ahead of time, then review and send it when due.
Remember: FollowLoop is currently assisted. It prepares, organises and checks the work; you still review before sending.
