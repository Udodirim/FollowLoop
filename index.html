How to Use FollowLoop

Start here the first time you use the app. Follow the journey from top to bottom — each step tells you what to do, what to expect, and where to go next.
1. Chat→ 2. Assignments→ 3. Follow-ups→ 4. Dashboard→ 5. Integrations→ 6. Settings→ 7. Content Queue→ 8. Content Packs
1

Start in Chat — this is the fastest route

FollowLoop is designed for tired, busy people. You do not need to learn the app first. Just tell it what you need or dump the information you already have.
  • Talk naturally: “I need to remind three people about Friday.”
  • If you are new, type: “I’m new. What can you do?” FollowLoop will explain the available options.
  • If you already have a list, copy and paste it from Excel/Google Sheets. FollowLoop can recognise assignment/reminder data and Content Queue data.
  • For assignments/reminders, useful columns are: Name, Email, WhatsApp, Event, Event Date, Event Time, Reminder Date, Reminder Stage, Reminder Message.
  • For Content Queue, use: Title, Date, Time, Channel, Caption/Message. Optional: Pack/Group, Link, Leader Email.
  • You can paste or upload an image. FollowLoop reads the text first and asks you to review before importing.
  • When you finish one activity and start another, the assistant starts a fresh conversation so the old task does not clutter the new one.
  • Dates, times, email addresses, WhatsApp numbers and links are checked before FollowLoop accepts them.
2

Review everything in Assignments

Assignments is where reminder details are checked before you act.
  • Confirm the person, email, WhatsApp number, event, date, time, reminder date, stage and message.
  • Times display in normal AM/PM format so they are easy to read.
  • Rows marked Needs attention are incomplete. Use Edit to correct them.
  • Use Delete if a reminder is no longer needed.
  • For several people, tick them or choose an audience under Bulk actions.
  • Choose Calendar, Email or WhatsApp, then click Review before opening the action.
3

Check Follow-ups

This page helps you see what has already been opened and what may still need attention.
  • Check the status under Calendar, Email and WhatsApp.
  • Use Edit if the reminder details need to change.
  • In the assisted version, “Opened” means FollowLoop prepared and opened the action — it does not mean the recipient definitely received or responded.
4

Return to the Dashboard

The Dashboard is your quick check-in point.
  • See how many assignments are ready or need attention.
  • See reminders due today.
  • Connect or reload a Google Sheet whenever you need to refresh your reminder data.
  • Scroll to the top of any page if needed — FollowLoop also takes you back to the top whenever you open a new section.
5

Understand Integrations

This explains what FollowLoop currently does with Google Sheets, Calendar, Gmail and WhatsApp.
  • Google Sheets can supply reminder or content data.
  • Calendar opens a prepared event.
  • Gmail opens a prepared email.
  • WhatsApp opens a prepared message.
  • Full background sending comes later when FollowLoop becomes autonomous.
6

Adjust Settings if needed

You normally do not need to change these every time.
  • Choose the default Calendar event duration.
  • Choose the preferred time for a future daily digest.
7

Advanced: Prepare recurring communication in Content Queue

Use this only when you want to prepare messages or posts ahead of time.
  • Add one item manually, or load a Content Queue Google Sheet.
  • Set the title, date, time, channel and message.
  • Add a link only if that communication actually needs one.
  • Add a leader reminder email if you want FollowLoop to prepare an email reminding the leader that the content is due.
  • When the time arrives, the item appears as Due now / Due today / Overdue.
  • In this assisted version, you still click Review or Email leader. Automatic background email comes with the autonomous version.
8

Advanced: Reuse repeated content with Content Packs

A Content Pack is simply a reusable group of Content Queue items.
  • Create a pack name, for example “Weekly Team Pack”.
  • Use that pack name when adding Content Queue items.
  • Next week, click Create next week and FollowLoop copies the pack’s queued items forward by 7 days.
  • You can then edit only what changed instead of creating everything again.
The simple idea: Give FollowLoop the information → check it → review what is due → act → follow up. Content Queue and Content Packs are optional advanced tools for recurring communication.
